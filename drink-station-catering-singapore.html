Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is in the drink series?

Even a drink should be an experience. Stations: Fruity tea boba DIY, Milk tea cloud DIY, Lemonade boba DIY, Dalgona cold foam. From $500 per 100 guests.

How many staff and how long is the service?

Standard service is 3 hours, and extra hours are $50 per hour. For 100 guests we bring 2 staff, for 200 to 400 guests 3 staff, and above 400 guests 4 staff.

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend booking at least 10 days in advance for standard catering and 15 days in advance for customised setups and themed events. Need something last-minute? Reach out and we will do our best, subject to availability.

Is Hummies halal-certified?

Hummies is not halal-certified, but we offer halal-friendly catering. We are happy to provide ingredient lists so your guests can make informed choices, and you may request Muslim staff for your event, subject to availability.

Do you do boba tea catering in Singapore?

Yes. Our drink series is a DIY boba tea catering station: fruity tea boba, milk tea cloud and lemonade boba. Guests build their own cup with our team on hand. From $500 per 100 guests.

Is it a live boba station or pre-made cups?

It is a live station. Guests pick their base, boba and toppings and make their own drink, so the station is part of the entertainment, not just a drink table.

Can the boba station suit weddings, birthdays and corporate events?

Yes. We set up the drink series for weddings, birthday parties, corporate events, school events and community events. Cups, stickers and menu boards can be matched to your theme.

Can I combine the drink station with a dessert station?

Yes. Many clients pair a drink station with a dessert live station such as ice cream soft serve, waffle wand or açaí. Message us on WhatsApp with your guest count and we will suggest a combination.
