Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you have a birthday party package?

Yes. Our little chefs’ party package is $500 for 30 kids and 3 hours, with three DIY stations: popcorn, soft serve or froyo, and mini donut or Dutch mini pancake. Name toppers and theme flags are free. For more guests or a different mix, message us and we will quote you.

How much does birthday party catering cost?

Hot dessert stations start from $600 for 100 guests, and snack, cold indulgent and drink stations from $500 for 100 guests, with a rate for each extra guest. Standard service is 3 hours, with a $100 delivery, manpower and setup fee per booking and $50 per extra hour. See our price guide or use the cost calculator.

Do you cater adult birthday parties like 21st and 30th birthdays?

Yes. We cater 21st, 30th and other milestone birthdays, surprise parties, graduation parties and family gatherings. Croffles, açaí, milk tea cloud and waffle wands are popular with older guests.

Do you cater kids’ birthday parties?

Yes. See our kids birthday party catering page for the little chefs’ party package with DIY popcorn, soft serve and mini donuts or Dutch mini pancakes.

Can you match our party theme?

Yes. We create personalised topping menu boards, custom posters and matching cutlery toppers for free. Share your theme and colours, and book at least 15 days ahead for themed setups.

How many guests can you cater for?

From about 30 to 500 guests. For 100 guests we bring 2 staff, for 200 to 400 guests 3 staff, and above 400 guests 4 staff. For fewer guests, see our small gathering options.

Can we mix stations?

Yes. Mix and match any stations for one event, for example a hot dessert with a drink. Every station comes with free customisation.

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend booking at least 10 days ahead for standard catering and 15 days ahead for customised menu boards and themed setups. Need something last-minute? Message us and we will do our best, subject to availability.

Can the station be set up outdoors?

Yes. Tell us your venue and setup area beforehand so we can make the necessary arrangements.
