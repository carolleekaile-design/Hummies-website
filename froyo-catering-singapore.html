About this station

Froyo, made your way

Froyo is where Hummies began, building a cup one topping at a time. At your event, choose a staff-served set package or a DIY package with unlimited toppings.

It suits corporate events, school events and parties where you want a lighter dessert.

How it works

Two tables, one live station

Most caterers set the food out on a table. At Hummies, your guests finish it themselves.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does froyo catering cost in Singapore?

The set package is $700 for 100 guests, staff-served with 1 drizzle and 3 toppings per cup. The DIY package is $800 for 100 guests with unlimited toppings. Prices shown are for the small cup, so WhatsApp us for a medium or large cup or for more than 100 guests. Standard service is 3 hours, with a $100 delivery, manpower and setup fee per booking.

What can guests choose for the froyo station?

Drizzles (choose 5): Cookie butter, Dark chocolate, Tiramisu, Nutella, Matcha, Peanut butter, Honey, Kinder Bueno, Condensed milk, Cookies & cream, Milk chocolate, Fruity jams. Toppings (choose 8): Granola, Oreo crumbs, Biscoff crumbs, Rose lychee crumbs, Taro crumbs, Double chocolate chip crumbs, Sea salt biscuit crumbs, Chocolate chips, Sprinkles, Fruity Pebbles, Rice Krispies, Corn flakes, Coco Pops, Red velvet crumbs, Strawberry, Banana, Mini marshmallow, Mango, Muscat grapes, Peach, Dragon fruit.

How many staff and how long is the service?

Standard service is 3 hours, and extra hours are $50 per hour. For 100 guests we bring 2 staff, for 200 to 400 guests 3 staff, and above 400 guests 4 staff.

Can I book froyo for fewer than 100 guests?

Yes. For small gatherings we offer a medium station from 50 guests. Set (staff-served, 1 drizzle and 3 toppings) is $600 for 50 guests, plus $10 per extra guest. DIY is $800 for 50 guests, plus $14 per extra guest. Want a different size? WhatsApp us and we will work out a package with you. For fewer than 50 guests, text us on WhatsApp.

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend booking at least 10 days in advance for standard catering and 15 days in advance for customised setups and themed events. Need something last-minute? Reach out and we will do our best, subject to availability.

Is Hummies halal-certified?

Hummies is not halal-certified, but we offer halal-friendly catering. We are happy to provide ingredient lists so your guests can make informed choices, and you may request Muslim staff for your event, subject to availability.

Can the station be set up outdoors?

Yes. Let us know your event location and setup area beforehand so we can make the necessary arrangements.

Can I customise the station for my event?

Yes. Customised toppers, stickers, cutlery, signage and menu boards are free of charge. Share your theme and we will match it.
