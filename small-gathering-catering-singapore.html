Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum number of guests?

Our small-gathering packages start at 50 guests, with a per-guest rate for each extra guest. For fewer than 50 guests, text us on WhatsApp and we will put together something that fits your group.

Can I get a different size for açaí, froyo or yogurt parfait?

Yes. WhatsApp us and we will work out a package with you.

What does the $100 delivery fee cover?

Delivery, manpower and setup are $100 per booking, and standard service is 3 hours. Additional service hours are $50 per hour.

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend booking at least 10 days in advance for standard catering and 15 days in advance for customised setups and themed events. Need something last-minute? Reach out and we will do our best, subject to availability.

Can I customise the station for my event?

Yes. Customised toppers, stickers, cutlery, signage and menu boards are free of charge. Share your theme and we will match it.

Hosting a small event?

Tell us the date, venue and number of guests and we will send a personalised quote.
