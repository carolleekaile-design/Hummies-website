Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is live station catering?

Instead of setting food out on a table, we bring a live station to your event. Guests collect a freshly made item from our preparation table, then build it their own way at the DIY topping bar.

What live stations do you offer?

Four series: hot dessert (waffle wands, Dutch mini pancakes, croffles, mini donuts), snack (popcorn bar, strawberry chocolate cups, pick-and-mix candy bar, s’mores), cold indulgent (açaí, froyo, ice cream soft serve, yogurt parfait) and drinks (fruity tea boba, milk tea cloud, lemonade boba).

How much does live station catering cost in Singapore?

Hot dessert stations start from $600 for 100 guests, snack stations from $500, cold indulgent stations from $500 and drink stations from $500. Standard service is 3 hours, with a $100 delivery, manpower and setup fee per booking and $50 per extra hour.

What events do you cater for?

Corporate events, school and university events, birthday parties, weddings, brand activations, community events, conferences, festive events and grand openings, from about 30 to 500 guests.

Do you offer a tasting?

Yes, a complimentary tasting session is available. Send an enquiry to book a date.

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend booking at least 10 days in advance for standard catering and 15 days in advance for customised setups and themed events. Need something last-minute? Reach out and we will do our best, subject to availability.

Is Hummies halal-certified?

Hummies is not halal-certified, but we offer halal-friendly catering. We are happy to provide ingredient lists so your guests can make informed choices, and you may request Muslim staff for your event, subject to availability.

Can the station be set up outdoors?

Yes. Let us know your event location and setup area beforehand so we can make the necessary arrangements.

Planning an event?

Tell us the date, venue and number of guests and we will send a personalised quote. A complimentary tasting is available.
