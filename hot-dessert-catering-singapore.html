Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is in the hot dessert series?

Grab a freshly made hot dessert from our preparation station, then head to the DIY topping bar to make it yours. Stations: Waffle wand DIY, Dutch mini pancake DIY, Croffle DIY, Mini donut DIY. From $600 per 100 guests.

How many staff and how long is the service?

Standard service is 3 hours, and extra hours are $50 per hour. For 100 guests we bring 2 staff, for 200 to 400 guests 3 staff, and above 400 guests 4 staff.

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend booking at least 10 days in advance for standard catering and 15 days in advance for customised setups and themed events. Need something last-minute? Reach out and we will do our best, subject to availability.

Is Hummies halal-certified?

Hummies is not halal-certified, but we offer halal-friendly catering. We are happy to provide ingredient lists so your guests can make informed choices, and you may request Muslim staff for your event, subject to availability.
