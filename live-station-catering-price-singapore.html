Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does live station catering cost in Singapore?

At Hummies, hot dessert stations start from $600 for 100 guests, and snack, cold indulgent and drink stations from $500 for 100 guests. Set packages are staff-served, and DIY packages let guests self-serve at the topping bar.

What is included in the price?

The station, ingredients, our staff, set-up and serving for 3 hours. Delivery, manpower and setup are $100 per booking, and each additional service hour is $50. Customisation such as personalised packaging, cutlery toppers, signage and menu boards is free.

What is the difference between a set package and a DIY package?

A set package is staff-served, so our team serves each guest. A DIY package lets guests self-serve at the topping bar, with more choice. For the cold indulgent series, the set package includes 1 drizzle and 3 toppings, and the DIY package has unlimited toppings.

Is there a cheaper option for smaller events?

Yes. Small-gathering packages start at 50 guests, for example $500 for a staff-served station with 2 hot dessert choices. For fewer than 50 guests, WhatsApp us and we will put something together.

Want an exact quote?

Send us your date, venue and number of guests and we will confirm the price. A complimentary tasting is available.
