FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the topper design really free?

Yes. Customised toppers, stickers, cutlery, signage and menu boards are free with every Hummies live station booking in Singapore.

Is this my final design?

No. This is a quick preview so we can see your idea. Our team turns it into a polished design with you and sends it for your approval before printing.

Can you match my party theme or colours?

Yes. Tell us your theme, colours or invitation and we will match them. You can also send a photo or a Pinterest idea on WhatsApp.

Can you use cartoon characters?

We design original doodles and illustrations. Licensed characters from other brands need the owner’s permission, so please ask us what we can do for your theme.

How far ahead should I book customised designs?

Please book at least 15 days ahead for customised designs, and 10 days ahead for standard catering.

What can you customise besides toppers?

Menu boards, topping and drizzle cards, cutlery, packaging, stickers, cart signage and flags in your theme.

Ready to plan your party?

Tell us the date, venue and number of guests and we will send a personalised quote.
