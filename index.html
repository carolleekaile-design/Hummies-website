Our story

It all started with a little cup of froyo ♡

Click here to read more

It all started with Carol and her love for froyo. 🍦

As a little girl, Carol loved visiting froyo shops and getting to build her own cup, choosing her toppings, drizzles and little extras one by one. But there were times when her family couldn’t afford such treats, so she would stand there and carefully think twice before choosing even one topping.

Years later, at just 20, Carol got the chance to turn that little memory into something of her own. With startup funding, she opened Hummies’ first store at Ngee Ann Polytechnic, creating a little space where students could build their own desserts with unlimited toppings, drizzles and crumbs.

No more having to choose just one. 🥹🍓

Then came another little question:

“Why should DIY desserts only be froyo?”

So she started experimenting with mini pancakes, long waffles on a stick, açaí bowls, croffles and more. Soon, students weren’t just coming to Hummies for dessert. They were coming to make something silly, pretty, messy and completely their own.

Friends would gather after class, take photos of their creations, share a laugh and have a little sweet break from school. ♡

And that became what Carol loved most about Hummies.

It was never really just about the dessert.

It was about giving people a little moment to choose, create, play and enjoy.

So when Carol noticed that catering often felt like simply receiving food, she wanted to bring the Hummies feeling with her, turning dessert catering into a little live experience where everyone gets to join in.

Our story · Carol, founder of Hummies

Meet Carol, the little heart behind Hummies ♡

She started Hummies at just 20, with a simple love for creativity and bringing people joy through food. 🥹

One of her favourite things is watching people light up as they create desserts their own way. From little topping choices to specially themed setups for different events, Carol hopes to make every Hummies experience feel a little more personal, a little more fun, and a little more memorable. 🫶🏻

Our story · Old Hummies branding

A little throwback to Hummies’ first few catering events ♡

Back then, Carol was still figuring things out, learning with every event and doing her best to bring her ideas to life. 🥹

After a few events, she realised she wanted to create something more polished and professional, so she took a leap and started building Hummies’ very own DIY catering cart. A little step that became part of Hummies’ journey to grow. 🫶🏻

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

What live stations do you offer?

Four series: hot dessert, snack, cold indulgent and drinks. You can pick several desserts for one station, or pair them with our drinks.

What events do you cater for?

Corporate events, weddings, birthdays, school events and private gatherings, from about 30 to 500 guests.

How much does a live station cost?

Hot dessert series starts from $600 for 100 guests and snack series from $500. Standard service is 3 hours, with a $100 delivery, manpower and setup fee per booking and $50 per extra hour. See the pricing table above.

Can I customise the station?

Yes. Customised toppers, stickers and menu boards are free of charge. Share your theme and we’ll match it. Please book at least 15 days ahead for customised setups.

Do you offer a tasting?

Yes, a complimentary tasting session. Send an enquiry to book a date.

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend booking at least 10 days in advance for standard catering and 15 days in advance for customised setups and themed events.
Need something last-minute? Feel free to reach out to us! We’ll do our best to accommodate your event, subject to availability. 🫶🏻

Can the station be set up outdoors?

Yes, our catering stations can be set up outdoors too! ♡ Do let us know your event location and setup area beforehand so we can make the necessary arrangements.

Is Hummies halal-certified?

Halal-friendly catering ♡
While Hummies is not Halal-certified, we’re happy to provide ingredient lists for your guests to review and make informed choices. You may also request Muslim staff for your event, subject to availability.
