Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you do a long waffle wand for weddings?

Yes. Our long waffle wand is a popular wedding dessert. It is made fresh at your event and decorated by guests at the DIY bar, with a menu designed for your wedding.

How much does a wedding waffle wand station cost?

The waffle wand starts at $600 for 100 guests for the staff-served set package and $700 for the DIY package, with $5 to $6 for each extra guest. Delivery, manpower and setup is $100 per booking.

Can the menu include our names and illustration?

Yes. We design personalised menu boards with your names, an illustration if you like, topping and drizzle cards and matching signage for free. Book at least 15 days ahead.

Can we mix the waffle wand with other stations?

Yes. Many couples add croffles, açaí or milk tea cloud. See our wedding dessert catering page.

Can the station be set up outdoors?

Yes. Tell us your venue and setup area beforehand so we can make the necessary arrangements.

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend booking at least 10 days ahead for standard catering and 15 days ahead for customised menu boards and themed setups. Need something last-minute? Message us and we will do our best, subject to availability.
