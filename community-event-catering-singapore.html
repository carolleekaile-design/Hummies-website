Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does catering for community events and family days cost?

Hot dessert stations start from $600 for 100 guests, and snack, cold indulgent and drink stations from $500 for 100 guests. Standard service is 3 hours, with a $100 delivery, manpower and setup fee per booking and $50 per extra hour. Small-gathering packages start at 50 guests.

How many guests can you cater for?

From about 30 to 500 guests. We bring 2 staff for 100 guests, 3 staff for 200 to 400 guests and 4 staff above 400 guests.

How many guests can you cater for?

From about 30 to 500 guests. For 100 guests we bring 2 staff, for 200 to 400 guests 3 staff, and above 400 guests 4 staff.

Can we mix stations?

Yes. You can mix and match any stations for one event, for example a hot dessert with a drink. Every station comes with free customisation.

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend booking at least 10 days in advance for standard catering and 15 days in advance for customised setups and themed events. Need something last-minute? Reach out and we will do our best, subject to availability.

Can the station be set up outdoors?

Yes. Let us know your event location and setup area beforehand so we can make the necessary arrangements.

Planning your event?

Tell us the date, venue and number of guests and we will send a personalised quote. A complimentary tasting is available.
